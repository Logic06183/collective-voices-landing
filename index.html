THE RIGHT TO HEALTH FOR ALL
IN SOUTH AFRICA

Collective Voices for Health Access is a coalition of civil society organisations, activists, healthcare workers, and researchers that advocates for the right to health for all, including migrants and refugees, and campaigns against health xenophobia and discrimination in South Africa.
